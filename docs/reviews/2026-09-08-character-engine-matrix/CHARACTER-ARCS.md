## The central relationship

Individual coherence means recognizing one's internal compass, choosing an appropriate lens, and owning what follows. Team coherence means making those different processes legible enough to cooperate. Neither means agreement on every interpretation, constant emotional regulation, or surrendering judgment to a chart.

Destiny supplies a latent orientation and the story's prana economy; choice governs the response, its consequences, and responsibility. Engine outputs supply proposed symbolic conditions. They cannot supply a character's moral verdict. A difficult period cannot excuse harm; a favorable period cannot certify an action. Prana expenditure belongs to the book's fiction unless an actual engine field and unit support a quantitative statement. These runs do not measure prana or consciousness.

## Progression and recurrence

Use Ch1–4 for first interruptions, Ch5–8 for failure and repair, Ch9–15 for routines under shared work, Ch16–20 for relapse and accountable revision, and Ch21–27 for perspective and transfer. This is an editorial organization, not proof that the manuscript already demonstrates each stage fully. The six-week return supports recurrence; one expedition cannot by itself establish a whole changed personality.

Add short ordinary-life recurrences at existing transitions: a meal, a disputed report, rest, asking for assistance, correcting a colleague, admitting a mistake without making the listener provide absolution. Personality and worldview should be inferred from repeated choices across contexts, not announced as levels. Readers encounter the practice through action first; companion reflection follows its introduction and remains freely reusable.

## Team protocol for a scene

One person states the concrete context. Each identifies what they can perceive, what remains uncertain, and what they are willing to do. Choose one primary lens; bring a second only when it answers a different question or tests an apparent agreement. Record disagreement without forcing one system to win. Choose a bounded act, identify who carries its cost, and return to the outcome. The other engines remain available rather than becoming simultaneous obligations.

Examples: use HD for decision-process differences, Panchanga for shared calendar context, Vimshottari for differing long periods, Gene Keys for contemplative pattern language after label validation. A timing interpretation and a personal response may differ; neither cancels consent or evidence. The unit of team coherence is a workable relationship among distinct judgments, not one synchronized mood.
