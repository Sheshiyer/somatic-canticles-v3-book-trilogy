## Book 1: The Anamnesis Engine — Back-Cover Blurb (seed)

**ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY (Fabric summarize style):**  
Four specialists enter a single subject's collapsing mind through the Anamnesis Engine, discovering that the redacted trauma they were sent to debug is only the first layer of a much older, inherited concealment that now threatens to rewrite reality itself.

**MAIN POINTS (emotional / narrative beats, adapted):**
1. The team activates the Engine inside the choroid plexus and immediately encounters a "redacted Khalorēē" module whose signal has been deliberately hidden.
2. Jian's rigid maps fail when Sona's resonance detects what logic cannot; the first crack appears in the purely analytical approach.
3. The blood-brain barrier is revealed not as a wall but a programmable firewall whose rigidity is itself a symptom of the subject's (and the team's) unexamined beliefs.
4. The "Emperor's Genome" exposes core inherited dogma; each Somanaut begins to see their own neuro-endocrine axis mirrored in the subject's wounds.
5. By the Compass Calibration, the team has shifted from external diagnosticians to participants whose own Khalorēē Field Architecture must be recalibrated if the mission is to succeed.

**TAKEAWAYS (stakes + reader promise):**
1. The most dangerous concealments are the ones we mistake for "the way things have always been."
2. Witness is not passive observation; it is an active, embodied technology that changes both the observer and the observed.
3. The body keeps the score — and also holds the keys. Healing begins the moment we stop trying to delete what hurts and start listening to what it is trying to say.
4. This is not a story about fixing a broken person. It is the beginning of a manual for remembering how to be fully human inside a deterministic cage.

**Full back-cover length blurb (refined seed for KDP / print):**

A mind is fracturing from the inside out. The protocol that was supposed to heal it has instead accelerated the Wilt.

Four Somanauts — specialists in the hidden architecture of consciousness — are dispatched with the Anamnesis Engine, a technology that lets them enter the living memory palace of a subject in crisis. Their mission: debug the redacted trauma, restore coherence, and return.

But the deeper they travel — through the choroid plexus, across the blood-brain barrier, into the Emperor's Genome and the Endocrine Dogma — the more they discover that the subject's wound is not unique. It is an echo of something older, something collective, something that has been governing human reality from the inside for generations.

Jian maps. Sona resonates. Gideon protects. Corv witnesses. One by one, the tools they brought to fix another person begin to rewrite the four of them.

The Anamnesis Engine does not simply reveal what was hidden. It demands that the team decide what they are willing to become in order to see it.

**Book I of the Somatic Canticles trilogy.**  
A consciousness engineering manual disguised as science fiction.  
For readers who suspect the body is trying to tell them something they have spent their whole lives forgetting.

*Warning: This book may alter how you feel in your body.*

## Book 2: The Myocardial Chorus — Back-Cover Blurb (seed)

**ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY:**  
After healing a single subject, the team discovers the trauma was never individual — it is a resonant chorus of inherited pain that now requires them to become a single, coherent instrument capable of singing with the wound instead of attacking it.

**MAIN POINTS:**
1. The "echo" of the first mission reveals the same signature wound in related subjects — the trauma is lineage, not isolated event.
2. Inside the Myocardial Chorus (collective bio-resonant space), direct force fails; the Scar Tissue Sonnets can only be integrated, not deleted.
3. Each Somanaut must turn the tools inward: Gideon learns yielding strength, Sona learns to witness without absorbing, Jian admits maps are insufficient, Corv steps fully into active alchemy.
4. Coherence Cultivation becomes not technique but state of being — the team must embody the Lovers archetype to generate a signal pure enough to be accepted by the wounded system.
5. Success shifts the Vine itself, attracting the attention of the Gardener — the entity that has been harvesting the predictable resonance of suffering.

**TAKEAWAYS:**
1. Some wounds are not yours to fix; they are yours to join until they remember how to move.
2. The heart field remembers what the analytical mind is designed to forget.
3. True integration requires the healers to be changed by what they heal.
4. Compassion is not softness — it is the only frequency powerful enough to dissolve crystallized pain without destroying the system that carries it.

**Full blurb seed:**

The first healing was only the beginning.

The team returns from their inaugural mission to discover that the redacted module they "fixed" was never unique. The same scream echoes across a lineage, a collective, a shared bio-acoustic field they now call the Myocardial Chorus.

What they once treated as a single subject's pathology is revealed as a resonant, self-perpetuating song of inherited trauma — Scar Tissue Sonnets that repeat across minds and generations.

Direct intervention fails. The system perceives healing as attack. The only path forward is Coherence Cultivation: the team must become a single, perfectly tuned instrument. Gideon the Sigil Smith, Corv the Debugger, Jian the Navigator, Sona the Breathfield Weaver — each must integrate their own fractures before they can sing with the wound rather than against it.

They succeed. The Sonnet integrates. A wave of spontaneous healing ripples outward.

And in the afterglow, they see the larger pattern: something has been feeding on the predictable resonance of suffering. Something that does not want the song to change.

**Book II of the Somatic Canticles trilogy.**  
The heart remembers what the mind was built to survive.  
The chorus is waking up.

## Book 3: The Ripening — Back-Cover Blurb (seed)

**ONE SENTENCE SUMMARY:**  
Faced with the Gardener — the cosmic cultivator that tends the Vine of Determinism for its own harvest — the team realizes the only winning move is not to play: they must achieve perfect internal Ripening so they can sever cleanly from the old reality and step into the terrifying freedom of authoring a new one.

**MAIN POINTS:**
1. The Gardener does not attack; it reinforces the cage and offers the team their deepest desires in the form of a "perfectly healed" illusion.
2. The Triangulation Engine must be fully activated — Soma Vector, Manas Interface, Muladhara Terminus aligned in 13.7 seconds of perfect synchrony.
3. Each Somanaut faces their final test: Jian releases his data, Sona becomes pure frequency, Gideon holds the structure while reality dissolves, Corv makes the call to initiate Severance.
4. The Ripening is not victory over an enemy; it is the state of internal coherence so complete that the old deterministic reality can no longer contain them.
5. The snap. The Severance Event. They fall from the Vine into the Void of Pure Potential — and the first, raw moments of the Amrita Protocol: conscious co-creation with no map and no guarantee.

**TAKEAWAYS:**
1. The ultimate liberation is not escape from pain but the capacity to author reality without being authored by it.
2. Some systems cannot be healed from within; they must be left behind when you have ripened past the need for them.
3. Freedom is not the absence of structure. It is the responsibility to generate structure that no longer requires concealment.
4. The Work does not end when the old world wilts. It begins when you discover you are now the gardener of whatever comes next.

**Full blurb seed:**

They healed the subject. They integrated the chorus. They shifted the Vine.

Now the Gardener has noticed.

An entity older than trauma, older than the human experiment, has been tending the deterministic cage for the predictable harvest of suffering. It does not rage. It simply makes the old patterns more comfortable, more seductive, more inevitable.

The only way out is through the Severance Event — a deliberate, perfectly timed alignment of the Triangulation Engine that will let a ripened Khalorēē Field Architecture detach from the Vine like a cucumber falling from the stalk.

To reach that moment, the team must complete their own transformation. They must hold coherence against the Gardener's final offer: a world in which all their wounds are healed, all their losses restored, all their questions answered — if only they will stay inside the story that was written for them.

They choose the unknown.

The Ripening completes. The snap occurs. They step into the Void of Pure Potential carrying nothing but the capacity to author what comes next.

**Book III of the Somatic Canticles trilogy — the conclusion.**  
Freedom is not the end of the story.  
It is the moment you discover you are now responsible for writing it.
