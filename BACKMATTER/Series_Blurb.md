# Somatic Canticles — Series Blurb (Final, from v2 master)

**27 Chapters. Three Books. One Transformation.**

In 2026, when the hidden protocols of consciousness begin to fail, four Somanauts enter the living architecture of the body with the Anamnesis Engine — The Vine. The Wilt. The Witness. — to diagnose, integrate, and sever humanity from an inherited reality that was never truly free.

**Book I: The Anamnesis Engine**  
A mind is fracturing from the inside out. The protocol that was supposed to heal it has instead accelerated the Wilt. Four specialists enter a single subject's collapsing mind, only to discover the wound is collective, ancient, and now rewriting reality itself.

**Book II: The Myocardial Chorus**  
The first healing was only the beginning. The trauma is a resonant chorus of inherited pain. The team must become a single, coherent instrument capable of singing with the wound — or be consumed by it.

**Book III: The Ripening**  
The Gardener has noticed. The only way out is the Severance Event. They must ripen into authors of a reality they themselves must now create.
