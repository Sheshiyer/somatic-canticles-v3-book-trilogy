## Ṣaḍ Darśana as Khalorēē Variant-Expression Profiles

[TRADITIONAL-SOURCE: Indian philosophy]

The six darśanas (schools of Indian philosophy) are not competing truth claims. They are six metabolic strategies for processing identity — six ways the Khalorēē (identity signature) can express itself through somatic experience. Each darśana corrects the others' blind spots. The integrated Somanaut hears all six.

### Nyaya — Logic / Validation
Identity through verification. The Nyaya Khalorēē processes every sensation before believing it. Strength: immune to gaslighting. Blind spot: cannot act on unverified intuition; paralyzed by ambiguity. Somatic fingerprint: tension in the jaw and temples, the body held in perpetual readiness to evaluate.

### Vaisheshika — Atomistic Categorization
Identity through decomposition. The Vaisheshika Khalorēē breaks experience into constituent parts, categorizing each sensation by type and origin. Strength: extraordinary pattern recognition. Blind spot: the whole is always more than the sum of categorized parts; misses gestalt. Somatic fingerprint: fingertip sensitivity, the need to touch and sort.

### Samkhya — Dualist Enumeration
Identity through distinction. The Samkhya Khalorēē maps the boundary between purusha (witness) and prakriti (matter/nature). Strength: clear discernment of what is self and what is other. Blind spot: the boundary is itself a construction; over-enumeration leads to dissociation. Somatic fingerprint: a quality of stillness behind the eyes, the watcher who forgets to blink.

### Yoga — Disciplined Practice
Identity through stilling. The Yoga Khalorēē processes experience through the lens of practice: every sensation is material for the work of stilling the mind. Strength: extraordinary equanimity under pressure. Blind spot: stillness can become avoidance; the stilled mind may refuse to engage. Somatic fingerprint: breath control so refined it becomes invisible, heart rate variability that responds to intention.

### Mimamsa — Ritual / Hermeneutics
Identity through interpretation. The Mimamsa Khalorēē processes experience as text to be read, ritual to be performed, meaning to be extracted. Strength: finds significance where others see noise. Blind spot: over-interpretation; the map becomes the territory. Somatic fingerprint: rhythmic micro-movements, the body performing meaning even in stillness.

### Vedanta — Non-Dual Inquiry
Identity through dissolution. The Vedanta Khalorēē processes experience by asking "who is experiencing this?" until the questioner dissolves. Strength: access to non-dual awareness, the ground of being. Blind spot: dissolution can become bypass; the absolute can be used to avoid the relative. Somatic fingerprint: a quality of transparency, as if the body were a window rather than a wall.

## Prana as Kinetic Expression of Khalorēē

[HOUSE-MODEL]

Prana is not "energy." It is the kinetic expression of the Khalorēē — the way identity signature moves through the biofield. Where the Khalorēē is the note, prana is the sounding of that note through tissue, breath, and attention. Prana is measurable: bioimpedance, heart rate variability, galvanic skin response. It is not mystical; it is the somatic fingerprint of who you are, made kinetic.

The Pancha Mahabhuta (five elements) describe the density gradients through which prana moves:

- **Earth (Prithvi):** Densest gradient. Prana moves slowly, with weight. Somatic correlate: bone, muscle, structural integrity.
- **Water (Jala):** Fluid gradient. Prana moves with adaptability. Somatic correlate: blood, lymph, emotional fluidity.
- **Fire (Agni):** Transformative gradient. Prana moves with heat and change. Somatic correlate: metabolism, digestion, transformative insight.
- **Air (Vayu):** Mobile gradient. Prana moves with speed and lightness. Somatic correlate: breath, nervous system, thought.
- **Ether (Akasha):** Subtlest gradient. Prana moves as pure potential. Somatic correlate: the biofield itself, the space in which all other gradients operate.
