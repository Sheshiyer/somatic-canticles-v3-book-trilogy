## Loka

[TRADITIONAL-SOURCE: Vedic cosmology]

A loka is not a place. It is a perceptual jurisdiction — a plane of experience accessible through coherence, not through travel. One does not *go to* a loka; one *attunes to* its particular density of awareness. The Vine exploits the confusion between position and plane: it convinces the subject that their current loka is the only one, that the coordinate they occupy is the totality of what exists.

The Anamnesis Engine does not transport. It tunes. When a subject enters the chamber, the Engine adjusts their coherence signature until it matches the loka where their wound was first inscribed. The subject perceives this as memory, but it is not retrieval — it is re-presence. The wound is not recalled; it is re-inhabited.

**Key distinction:** Attunement is bidirectional. The loka also attunes to the subject. This is why the Engine requires witness: the witness holds the subject's native coherence while the subject's awareness extends into the wound-loka. Without the witness, the subject may not return — not because they are lost, but because they have attuned so completely to the wound-loka that their native plane no longer recognizes them.

## Tycho Frame

[HOUSE-MODEL]

Jian's diagnostic model, named for Tycho Brahe's geo-heliocentric compromise: the sun orbits the earth, but all other planets orbit the sun. Brahe could see that the Copernican model was correct, but he could not abandon the fixed earth. His frame was a blind-spot heuristic — a way of organizing data that preserved the observer's centrality at the cost of truth.

The Tycho Frame in the Anamnesis Engine is Jian's tool for mapping how subjects mistake position for essence. The subject believes they are the fixed center of their own experience; the Vine exploits this certainty. When Jian runs a Tycho diagnostic, he is not measuring what the subject perceives — he is measuring the gap between what the subject perceives and what the subject's coherence signature actually contains.

**The trap:** The more precisely a subject can describe their own position, the more completely they are trapped by it. Precision without astonishment is the Vine's favorite food. Jian's genius is that he uses precision *against* the trap: he makes the subject's position so precisely described that the subject can finally see it as a position, not a truth.

## Philosopher's Stone — B1 Row

The Locative Frame is the entry condition for the tri-vector lock. Before Identity Signature (B2) or Authorship Protocol (B3) can activate, the subject must stabilize their Locative Frame: they must know where they stand without believing that where they stand is all there is.

The B1 crucible is where determinism is first mistaken for essence. The subject who has been told their wound defines them, their lineage condemns them, their position limits them — this subject must first learn that position is not essence. The Locative Frame, stabilized, becomes the ground from which the other two vectors can launch.

**Tri-vector lock sequence:** Locative Frame (B1) → Identity Signature (B2) → Authorship Protocol (B3) → 13.7-second phase-shift → Philosopher's Stone state. The lock is not sequential in time but sequential in dependency: each vector requires the prior vector's stability.
