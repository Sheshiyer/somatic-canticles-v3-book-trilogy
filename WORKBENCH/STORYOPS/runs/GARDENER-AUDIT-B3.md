## 3. FAIL / WARN Remediation

**FAIL: none.** Zero sneering, gloating, threatening, or suffering-enjoyment lines anywhere in B3.

**WARN-1 — Ch16 L769:** "burning the seeds before they could sprout, ensuring that nothing new would ever grow to challenge the old order."
- Issue: "challenge the old order" assigns the Gardener a defensive-political motive. Elsewhere it prunes from conservation logic, never to suppress challengers.
- The line is inside the team's despair spiral (tools useless, narrative-level threat), so it functions as projection — but nothing on-page marks it as such.
- Remediation (if touched): soften to keep the fear while dropping the motive, e.g. "ensuring that nothing unplanned would ever grow at all" — same dread, no politics. Optional; acceptable as-is if read as character POV.

## 4. Overall Assessment

**GATE: PASS.** The Gardener maintains sorrowful necessity across the trilogy with remarkable consistency.

- **Dialogue is uniformly offer-shaped.** Every Gardener utterance in B3 is an offer (story, peace, certainty, safety, rest) or a statement of creed ("That is what care is") or regret ("I will not be able to prevent it"). No insults, no threats, no triumphalism — even at refusal.
- **Menace comes from sincerity and scale, as designed.** The audit's strongest confirmations: Ch17 L141 ("I understand, and I must still tend"), Ch24 L278–280 ("The sorrow in the voice was real. That was the worst part."), and the four personalized premises in the countdown (L539–549), which weaponize earned relief rather than fear.
- **The text self-polices.** Ch17 L219 ("It did not smile, nor did it frown") and Ch24 L280 explicitly instruct the reader on the intended register — useful anchors if later drafts drift.
- **The new bridge content (Ch24 manifestation + premises) is the strongest Gardener writing in the book** — the counterclaim through "felt premises" rather than speech is exactly the function-believing-it-is-care the gate requires.
- One WARN (Ch16 L769) and one cross-book register question (Ch4's humanized Gardener) are the only items carried forward.
