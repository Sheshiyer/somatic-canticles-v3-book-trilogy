## Shared rule

Every character carries every sheath layer: material/apparatus, vital rhythm, felt meaning, discernment/model, and belonging/spaciousness. The revision should not assign Jian=model, Sona=feeling, Gideon=boundary, Corv=witness as permanent taxonomy. Those are entry habits. Transformation appears when a person notices that another layer or another person's lens has a valid claim and changes action under cost.

The reusable transformation chain is:

1. Useful defense.
2. Short-term benefit.
3. Hidden cost.
4. Recognition under pressure.
5. Changed attempt.
6. Relapse or overcorrection.
7. Repair with consent/cost ownership.
8. Transfer into a different scene.
9. Ordinary pleasure that proves life widened without becoming trophy.

## Jian

- Useful defense: precision, model integrity, private records, timing gates, route archives.
- Short-term benefit: he prevents vague care from becoming magical thinking and keeps dangerous evidence reviewable.
- Hidden cost: he can treat route completion, clean data, or sender timing as sufficient authority, especially when another person pays the delay or transfer cost.
- Recognition: Chapter 6 lets Sona's wrong-scale objection change his scanner use; Chapter 14 makes receiver readiness irreducible to route completion; Chapter 25 lets an unfiled/private note remain private.
- Changed attempt: ask before letting a route, object, or graph assign cost. Record the disagreement as part of reality, not as a flaw in the data.
- Relapse/overcorrection: over-explain the lesson, keep a trace too private, or turn a beautiful threshold into a new rule.
- Repair: visible log amendment that names carrier/receiver consent and leaves unresolved lines unresolved.
- Transfer: Chapter 6 shard custody -> Chapter 14 receiver timing -> Book 3 body-cost ledger and no-retrospective-claim rules.
- Positive decisive action: keep an inconvenient outlier visible when it costs speed/status.
- Ordinary pleasure: exactness with delight, not exactness as armor.

## Sona

- Useful defense: felt meaning, tonal climate, body-level signal, willingness to carry what others cannot yet hear.
- Short-term benefit: she finds the living carrier beneath noise and prevents procedural care from missing grief.
- Hidden cost: invisible labor; relief can become anesthesia; her sensing can become unvoted team infrastructure.
- Recognition: Chapter 7 exposes over-soothing; Chapter 14 makes receiver readiness speak before thought arrives; Chapter 25 shows remaining does not require Joy's signal.
- Changed attempt: ask another person to stop her when her gift becomes smoothing; name the body-cost difference before a vote seals itself.
- Relapse/overcorrection: withdraw all warmth to avoid coercion, or carry discomfort silently because naming it would slow the room.
- Repair: let anger, rest, and private feeling remain without making them proof of either failure or enlightenment.
- Transfer: Chapter 2/4 invisible subsidy -> Chapter 6 body-scale correction -> Chapter 7 consentful stopping -> Chapter 14 receiver timing -> Chapter 27 look without entering.
- Positive decisive action: protect a receiver's not-yet from being translated as resistance or inefficiency.
- Ordinary pleasure: cups, water, laughter, warmth, local weather, rest.

## Gideon

- Useful defense: perimeter, Klei, relation checks, refusal to let emergency become unbounded.
- Short-term benefit: people survive because someone is watching the edge and asking what can be carried.
- Hidden cost: protection can reduce life; availability can be mistaken for neutral custody; he can be thanked by outcomes for narrowing everyone.
- Recognition: Chapter 4 distinguishes load assessment from permission; Chapter 6 needs explicit Klei custody consent; Chapter 23 shows the seduction of managed safety; Chapter 27 lets the opening remain an opening.
- Changed attempt: guard passage rather than against passage; give carrier conditions before accepting custody.
- Relapse/overcorrection: seal first and investigate later, or refuse any boundary for fear of becoming controlling.
- Repair: name fear without making it command the group; accept support without making support sovereign.
- Transfer: Chapter 3 scar as native signature -> Chapter 4 load assessment -> Chapter 6 Klei custody -> Chapter 14 non-emergency perimeter -> Chapter 27 dipped boundary.
- Positive decisive action: condition custody and honor the abort/no-appeal condition when his edge detects inward cutting.
- Ordinary pleasure: usable exits, water verified as water, dry protocols, openings that stay open.

## Corv

- Useful defense: witness, sequence, meaning, exact refusal of cheap story.
- Short-term benefit: he can keep pain from becoming noise and can preserve coordinates without immediate conquest.
- Hidden cost: witness can become possession; beauty can make suffering usable before the person is safe.
- Recognition: Chapter 15 says he is not the singular narrator; Chapter 22 makes him admit wanting exquisite closure; Chapter 27 lets him close his eyes when watching becomes possession.
- Changed attempt: ask permission before leaving a seed; pause before placing a shard into another person's lattice; let a good line stay unimproved.
- Relapse/overcorrection: reject all meaning as theft, or return to leadership without restitution because the sentence is beautiful.
- Repair: concrete restitution, right to refuse reconciliation, witness that remains answerable to others.
- Transfer: Chapter 5 delayed anomaly -> Chapter 6 mute witness risk -> Chapter 8 seed permission -> Chapter 15 no singular narrator -> Chapter 22 no retrospective redemption -> Chapter 27 unclaimed image.
- Positive decisive action: refuse to turn Mira, Anvel, or the team's exhaustion into proof that the suffering was necessary.
- Ordinary pleasure: beautiful but unsealed endings, dry jokes, belonging without capture, images left unclaimed.

- No trajectory relies on fixed engine ownership, deterministic chart motive, or inevitable transformation.
