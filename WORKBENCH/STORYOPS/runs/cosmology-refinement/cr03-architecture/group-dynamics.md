# CR-03.02 group dynamics

Status: complete planning artifact. This does not rewrite manuscript prose.

## Coalition rule

The group does not become a single integrated mind. It becomes a coalition that can preserve different lenses under pressure. Teamwork is the practice of not converting another person's lens into one's own preferred layer.

## Anti-cliche team tests

A scene fails if it resolves because everyone suddenly agrees, because private thoughts become shared property, because one character's gift becomes the group's master lens, or because suffering becomes useful enough to justify itself.

A scene passes if an objection changes action, a cost has a bearer, dissent survives, privacy survives membership, and the next recurrence is harder or different.

## Scaling rule from CR-02

Every meaningful transformation must identify:

1. Pull.
2. Interpretation.
3. Action.
4. Cost-bearer.
5. Dissent.
6. Recurrence.
7. What remains private or unresolved.
