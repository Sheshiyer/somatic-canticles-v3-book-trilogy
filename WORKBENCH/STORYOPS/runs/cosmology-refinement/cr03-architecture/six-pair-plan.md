# CR-03.02 six pair plan

Status: complete planning artifact. This does not rewrite manuscript prose.

## Shared pair rule

A pair arc must include setup, misreading, negotiated action, aftermath, and later change. The pair does not need emotional agreement, private disclosure, or permanent dependence. It needs a visible change in action caused by one person receiving the other's lens without being converted into it.

## Jian / Sona

- Setup: Jian trusts waveform, route, and model; Sona hears current, pulse, grief, and receiver readiness.
- Misreading: Jian can treat Sona's state as mood contamination; Sona can treat Jian's timing need as avoidance of feeling.
- Negotiated action: Chapter 6 scanner turns toward her objection; Chapter 7 she asks him to stop her if smoothness becomes suppression; Chapter 14 receiver readiness corrects sender timing.
- Aftermath: the record must include Sona's cost without making her the field's emotional infrastructure.
- Later change: Jian records ordinary embodied detail and leaves some notes private; Sona lets a signal not arrive before remaining.
- Proposed beat: in Book 1 revisions, add one short consequence where Jian asks whether a trace affects Sona before filing it as safe.

## Jian / Gideon

- Setup: Jian gives precise models; Gideon gives perimeter and survival conditions.
- Misreading: Jian can treat Gideon's fear as unsophisticated risk management; Gideon can treat Jian's acceleration as breach.
- Negotiated action: Gideon demands honest translation of House language; Jian names reduction as pruning. Chapter 6 candidate lets Gideon condition Klei custody.
- Aftermath: the log becomes more honest and slower; the perimeter remains useful without becoming command.
- Later change: rules can be proposed without tyranny; boundaries can hold openings.
- Proposed beat: give Gideon one operational veto that Jian records without relabeling as inefficiency.

## Jian / Corv

- Setup: both are precision workers. Jian protects record integrity; Corv protects meaning from premature closure.
- Misreading: Jian may suspect Corv of poetic vagueness; Corv may suspect Jian of conquest by model.
- Negotiated action: Corv asks what Jian treats detours as; Jian recalibrates survival logic. Jian saves AM-38; Corv warns that privacy matters.
- Aftermath: coordinates remain useful but not sovereign.
- Later change: AM-38 points to the moving gap; Corv refuses a beautiful sequence that would make the record morally comfortable.
- Proposed beat: in Book 2/3 bridge revisions, let Jian and Corv disagree over whether a saved trace should be opened, with both partly right.

## Sona / Gideon

- Setup: Sona carries tone and body-level meaning; Gideon carries boundary and load assessment.
- Misreading: Gideon can make care smaller than life; Sona can make care total intake.
- Negotiated action: Chapter 4 load assessment respects her decision rights; Chapter 25 fear is named without becoming command; Chapter 27 defines guarding passage rather than guarding against passage.
- Aftermath: both forms of labor become visible as chosen effort, not default team infrastructure.
- Later change: Sona can look without entering; Gideon can stand at an opening without sealing it.
- Proposed beat: in Chapter 6/12 revisions, make one moment where Sona declines to carry and Gideon does not automatically take over.

## Sona / Corv

- Setup: Sona protects present feeling; Corv protects witness and sequence.
- Misreading: Corv can turn Sona's warning into part of his story; Sona can become the call-back function for his temptation.
- Negotiated action: Sona calls him back from beautiful closure; Corv admits wanting it and refuses to use Mira.
- Aftermath: her intervention must remain her action, not material for his redemption.
- Later change: Corv does not translate Sona's tree-boundary experience; Sona lets his unclaimed meaning remain.
- Proposed beat: add one restitution beat where Corv changes practical behavior after Sona's correction rather than only saying the better sentence.

## Gideon / Corv

- Setup: Gideon carries perimeter; Corv carries witness.
- Misreading: Gideon can rescue Corv into a clean danger story; Corv can make Gideon's fear narratively noble.
- Negotiated action: Gideon does not rescue Corv from the beautiful offer; Corv thanks him for not making refusal permission to pry. Corv later names passage as not failure of protection.
- Aftermath: both learn non-action can be active care.
- Later change: Gideon guards passage; Corv stops watching when watching becomes possession.
- Proposed beat: in late Book 3 compression, preserve one unsentimental non-intervention instead of replacing it with explanatory dialogue.

## Verification notes

- All six pairs have setup, misreading, negotiated action, aftermath, later change, and proposed beat.
- No pair requires permanent harmony, total disclosure, or emotional agreement.
- Decision rights stay local to carrier/receiver/cost bearer.
