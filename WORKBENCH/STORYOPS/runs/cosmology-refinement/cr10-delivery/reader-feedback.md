Status: prepared; no reader study executed in this phase.

## What to observe

- Can a reader name the concrete choice in Chapter 6 without needing the engine glossary?
- Can a reader tell whose cost changes in Chapter 14 and what remains unresolved?
- Does the post-ripening boundary language in Chapter 24 read as agency rather than boundary erasure?
- Does the Chapter 26 seed reference now point clearly to the Compass Calibration lineage?

## What not to score

- Do not equate streaks, report depth, birth-chart familiarity, or worldview agreement with transformation.
- Do not score a reader's inner development from app usage.
- Do not require readers to expose private reflections to prove comprehension.

## How findings re-enter StoryOps

Each future change needs a concrete observed problem, the affected source span, a scoped hypothesis, and an explicit stop condition. Confusion and rejection stay as evidence; they are not automatically defects.
