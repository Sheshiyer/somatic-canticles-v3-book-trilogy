# CR-00 contract rationale

The previous StoryOps contract rejected `non_additive_or_compressing_change` unconditionally. That made the cosmology refinement impossible to execute truthfully because this campaign often needs compression: repeated explanation should become action, consequence, or silence when the scene already carries the meaning.

The replacement rule is narrower: compression is permitted when it is justified and preserves consequential choice, object continuity, and scene order. It is still rejected when a protected decision disappears or when shorter prose is treated as success by itself.

Deterministic proof:

- Justified compression with `Gideon said yes` preserved: accepted.
- Compression that removes `Gideon said yes`: rejected.

This proof does not replace literary review. It only removes a false mechanical blocker while keeping scene-spine protection in force.
