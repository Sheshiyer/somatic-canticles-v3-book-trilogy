## Required candidate movement

A valid candidate should add or sharpen a costly second handoff where:

- The sender is ready before the receiver is.
- A failed relay costs time, comfort, political margin, or body-load.
- Someone names that readiness cannot substitute for consent.
- The next successful relay changes because of that objection.
- The chamber learns by iteration rather than by a perfect team insight.

## Evaluation rubric

Advance a candidate only if:

- Different readiness stays visible.
- Receiver consent changes the handoff mechanics.
- Failure remains meaningful and costly.
- No one is humiliated into wisdom.
- The Chapter 6 lesson recurs in a new form rather than being restated.
- Repetitive explanation is reduced only where action carries the meaning.

Reject a candidate if it creates smooth harmony, unanimous interpretation, or a clean proof that the team has transcended conflict.
