## Decision 1: Lens 13 is bounded authorship

Lens 13 means the subject stops being merely read by a diagnostic frame and begins authoring a response from available trajectories. It is not total prediction, total access, moral purification, or fate.

Evidence:

- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-22-The-Perfect-World.md:114` says Lens 13 is the writing surface where the subject starts writing; trajectories are available rather than fated.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-24-The-Final-Procedure.md:230` protects distinct witnesses and rejects a single witness wearing four faces.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-24-The-Final-Procedure.md:867` shows old jurisdiction failing at the edges, which supports severance from inherited command rather than severance from relation.
- Noesis Writer guidance preserves destiny and choice, practical usefulness, fictional mechanism, and empirical claims as separate layers.

Working rule:

Lens 13 ends automatic jurisdiction of a particular inherited command. It does not end error, privacy, relationship, bodily cost, humor, fatigue, or the need to choose well later.

Later prose fence:

Candidates may clarify Lens 13 language where it sounds like omniscience, purified coherence, or permanent attainment. They must preserve the scene's operational stakes and distinct witnesses.

## Decision 2: Ripening removes pruneable edges, not living boundaries

The Chapter 24 phrase "A fully ripened subject had no edges" should be treated as a local technical claim about the Gardener's jurisdiction. The safer campaign language is: no unowned, coherence-thinning, pruneable edge remains for the Gardener to claim as failed presence.

Evidence:

- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-24-The-Final-Procedure.md:466` says "Hold your own edges," directly preserving personal boundaries during the procedure.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-25-The-Void-of-Pure-Potential.md:380` keeps Gideon's fear of a place without edges as a lived risk, not final doctrine.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-26-The-Architecture-of-New-Reality.md:255` defines perimeter as a verb learning grammar, able to hold and move.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-27-The-New-Beginning.md:219` says a boundary can shelter growth without dictating its final form.

Working rule:

Use three edge categories:

- Pruning edge: exploitable thinning the Gardener can claim.
- Living boundary: answerable limit that preserves agency and relation.
- Opening edge: passage or future contact that remains possible without conquest.

Later prose fence:

Chapter 24 candidates should test a small clarifier near the risky line: "no edge the Gardener could claim" or equivalent. Do not flatten Chapter 25's fear or Chapter 27's boundary/opening material; they are canon answers, not contradictions.

## Decision 3: Sheaths are all-person dynamic layers

The five-layer sheath model is an editorial diagnostic for scene work, not a fixed character taxonomy, Selemene output, or traditional equivalence claim.

Working layers:

- Material body and apparatus: sleep, pain, touch, equipment limits, physical continuity.
- Vital effort and rhythm: pace, recovery, prana expenditure as fictional economy, fatigue.
- Felt meaning and attachment: grief, love, shame, comfort, aesthetic hunger, belonging.
- Discernment and model: explanation, diagnostic maps, inference, strategy, abstraction.
- Belonging and spaciousness: membership, privacy, silence, home, room for difference.

Evidence:

- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/inputs/CRITIQUE.md:85` labels this as an editorial translation rather than computed canon.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-27-The-New-Beginning.md:608` through `611` releases each character from old totalizing burdens without claiming they lose the layer itself.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-26-The-Architecture-of-New-Reality.md:342` maps different corrections into body sites without making those sites permanent identities.

Working rule:

Every major character carries all layers. A scene becomes team-work when one person notices that another person's layer is diverging from their own explanation and changes action accordingly.

Later prose fence:

Do not write scenes where Corv equals witness, Sona equals feeling, Jian equals model, and Gideon equals boundary as permanent roles. Those are entry habits and temptations; the revision must show cross-layer surprise, relapse, repair, and transfer.

## Decision 4: Destiny and choice stay paired

Destiny is latent blueprint, pull, and prana-economy pressure. Choice is how a person interprets that pull, acts, owns consequences, and repairs harm. Destiny never excuses harm or proves a favorable outcome was morally correct.

Evidence:

- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/CONTEXT.md:8` preserves destiny and choice together.
- Noesis Writer lived-integration guidance says destiny does not command adherence or absolve action.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-26-The-Architecture-of-New-Reality.md:40` says old habits are familiar and not the whole blueprint; neither habit nor possibility can take the next step for Corv.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-27-The-New-Beginning.md:101` through `107` keeps consent in the coordinate system and refuses punishment.

Working rule:

In scene briefs, separate pull, interpretation, action, effect, and ownership. A character can feel destiny without receiving moral permission. A character can be responsible for response without being blamed for every condition around them.

Later prose fence:

Do not remove destiny language globally. Test only passages where destiny sounds like coercive prediction, spiritual debt accounting, or exemption from repair.

## Decision 5: Shared witness does not erase privacy

The team can perceive relation-state, body-cost signals, readiness, and addressability. They cannot automatically access another person's interior contents, private pain, or unspent thoughts.

Evidence:

- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-25-The-Void-of-Pure-Potential.md:54` says Sona receives the team, not personalities first or private pains.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-25-The-Void-of-Pure-Potential.md:640` says a thought can remain unspent and a feeling private without becoming secret.
- `CHAPTERS/book_3/Chapter-27-The-New-Beginning.md:421` shows Sona meeting another rhythm at a boundary instead of pulling it into herself.
- `CHAPTERS/book_2/Chapter-15-The-Witness-Integration.md` already resists no-fusion and effortless merger.

Working rule:

Knowing another's state is less than understanding them; understanding them is less than having access to all of them. Good teamwork includes not using available information.

Later prose fence:

When revising witness scenes, preserve privacy as membership-compatible. A withheld thought, unsent note, declined explanation, or unclassified feeling can be an active team contribution.

## Decision 7: Book and companions share situations, not authority

The novel is a complete encounter. Web, mobile, Selemene, source packs, wiki, audio, and later app flows may carry practice, context, and return paths, but they cannot replace the story or certify reader transformation.

Evidence:

- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/inputs/ECOSYSTEM-PLAN.md:5` says a reader can complete the novel without an account or report.
- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/inputs/ECOSYSTEM-PLAN.md:54` separates practice identity from chapter identity.
- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/inputs/ECOSYSTEM-PLAN.md:65` says practice access follows saved introduction event; attempts, reflections, reading percentage, timing, or reports do not prove spiritual achievement.
- `WORKBENCH/STORYOPS/plans/cosmology-refinement/inputs/ECOSYSTEM-PLAN.md:82` and `83` keep web/mobile evidence dated and unverified for current production.

Working rule:

The shared meaning unit is a situated dilemma: stimulus, interpretation, choice, consequence, return. Each surface compresses that unit differently. Mobile can offer a small action; web can unpack interpretations; Selemene can provide an optional symbolic lens; the book carries ambiguity and consequence.

Later prose fence:

Do not move exercises into the novel. Do not build app state or companion mappings from chapter numbers alone. Do not let a favorable engine reading or completed practice become proof that a character or reader has transformed.
