# CR-08.02 web exploration pilot — B1C6 shard custody

## Story context

Chapter 6 tests a false shortcut: the team can agree to retain the shard, but that does not automatically mean Gideon's Klei has agreed to carry it. The meaningful change is not better vocabulary; it is a changed action before transfer.

## Expanded reflection

- Stimulus: a risky artifact or decision appears useful.
- Interpretation: the group treats retention as the main question.
- Hidden cost: one carrier may absorb forensic, metabolic, memory, or social risk.
- Owned action: the carrier names a condition; the group records it.
- Consequence: the decision remains usable and imperfect.
- Return: later scenes test whether conditions are remembered when timing pressure increases.

## Optional lens context

A Selemene/Noesis lens may ask what layer is being asked to carry cost: body, rhythm, feeling, model, or belonging. The lens does not decide whether the choice is correct. It only helps the reader ask where consent, cost, and recurrence have separated.
