# CR-08.02 mobile encounter pilot — B1C6 shard custody

Situation: a group wants to retain a difficult object/decision, but one person or instrument may carry the cost.

Short form:

1. Notice the proposed action.
2. Ask: who carries the cost if this leaks, fails, or returns later?
3. Ask the carrier/receiver for a condition, refusal, or review trigger.
4. Record the condition without turning it into praise.
5. Return later and ask whether the condition was honored.

Reader prompt: Where in your current situation are you treating availability as consent?

No birth profile, engine score, or practice outcome is required to understand this encounter.
