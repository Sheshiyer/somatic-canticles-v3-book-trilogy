# Campaign review rubric

Retain the existing seven StoryOps gates: Aletheos, PubMed precision, Alex Grey embodiment, purposeful opacity, moral premise, Gardener integrity, and red flags. Record pass/warn/fail/deferred with exact passage evidence and critic identity. Numerical style floors are aids, not proof of reader experience. No default no-regression rule should force a useless phrase merely to retain a keyword percentage; document intentional tradeoffs for editorial review.

Add the following scene/arc tests where applicable. A quiet transition need not perform every test; scope each brief to its actual job.

| Test | Evidence the critic must supply | Failure example |
|---|---|---|
| Distinct attention | Same stimulus produces different specific observations or stakes | Four interchangeable philosophical speeches |
| Useful defense | Old habit brings a real short-term benefit | A flaw exists only to be disowned |
| Negotiated action | A colleague’s objection changes what happens | Agreement is merely announced |
| Burden and agency | Person bearing effort/risk has a legible relation to the decision | Consent vocabulary disguises assigned labor |
| Positive/negative movement | Gain, loss, relapse or overcorrection has consequences | Every insight earns instant improvement |
| Recurrence | Later different situation tests a changed response | Repeated motif treated as established habit |
| Layered person | Body, effort, attachment, model and belonging can diverge | One permanent sheath/engine defines the person |
| Group dynamics | Coalition, absence, status or work allocation affects action | Six pair charts treated as the whole team |
| Adequate action | A useful imperfect commitment can be made | Refusal automatically equals wisdom |
| Ordinary life | Voice, humor, preference, pleasure or care survives | Every exchange becomes an ethical hearing |
| Faithful compression | Choice, agent, cost, uncertainty and return remain | Cutting the consequence but retaining its lesson |
| Epistemic fidelity | Returned fact, house model and interpretation distinguishable | Birth-cycle values presented as measured fatigue |

## Acceptance

A candidate needs source parity, exact diff, no unreviewed continuity break, no unsupported imported engine claim, and independent literary review. It must improve its declared target or fix a concrete defect while preserving protected beats. Record deliberate exceptions, including register WARNs, by passage. Never translate completion of this checklist into spiritual/moral assessment of a reader or character.

Use blind A/B excerpts where practical, then read complete chapters and neighbors. Ask the reader what each person wanted, what changed the decision, who paid, what remained disputed, and whether the prose felt lived or explained. Keep counter-readings. A writer does not grade their own candidate as the final authority.

## Wave close

The author receives exact candidate hashes, concise findings, unresolved alternatives and expected downstream effects. Human acceptance selects the text; a separate promotion action places it in CHAPTERS. Source exports, remote merge, publication and deployed companion behavior each require their own receipt.
