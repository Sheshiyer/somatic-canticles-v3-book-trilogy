## Critical path

CR-00 → CR-01 → CR-02 pilot → CR-03 architecture → CR-04 Book 1 → CR-05 Book 2 → CR-06 Book 3 → CR-07 convergence.

After CR-07, manuscript exports/delivery (CR-10) can proceed independently of companion content (CR-08 → CR-09). CR-10’s optional companion release requires CR-09; book-only release does not. Early read-only engine/app research may proceed during the literary phases, but implementation follows its approved content/behavior contract.

Planning is authorized; task completion must be earned through the named receipts. Canon decisions in CR-01 and candidate acceptance at wave close require concrete review, not repeated permission to inspect or draft. No global approval question is needed to begin CR-00.
